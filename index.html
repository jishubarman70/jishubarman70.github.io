ABOUT THE ARTIST

Jishu Barman is an independent music artist from Nalbari, Assam, India. His passion for music began in childhood, with a dream of creating his own songs and melodies. Although he once felt that his voice was not suitable for singing, his love for music never faded. He continued exploring music and eventually discovered his creative path through AI-powered music production. In 2026, he began his journey as an independent music artist, creating Assamese romantic, emotional and melodic songs. His vision is to express emotions through music, connect with listeners and share his creativity with the world.
